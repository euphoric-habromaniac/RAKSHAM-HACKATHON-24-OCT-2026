CallTimeline is the footer strip: a row of tick marks standing for the minutes of a call, with a marker at the current page.

Provide the page number and total pages. The strip is `ink-faint` ticks at an 8px pitch, one tick taller every fifth, with a `genuine` marker on the current page's position and a short `synthetic` dashed segment near the end. The source line sits to its left in `caption`; the page number to its right.

Use it once per page at `space-8` from the bottom edge, 24px high. Do not recolour it per section, and do not add labels to the ticks.
