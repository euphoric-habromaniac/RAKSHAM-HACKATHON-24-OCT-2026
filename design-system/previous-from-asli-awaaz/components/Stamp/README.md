Stamp is a small rotated outline label that marks a figure or claim as an assumption, a target or an illustration.

Provide the label text (four words or fewer) and an optional rotation between -3 and +3 degrees. Use one stamp per figure, set next to the figure and never over it.

The standard labels are "Assumption", "Target, not a result", "Illustrative" and "Not yet tested". "Target, not a result" is the one wording for every target. Set them in `label` with a 2px outline in `ink-soft` on `paper`, `radius-sm`. A stamp is neutral: `ink-soft` or `ink`, never `genuine` or `synthetic`, because a stamp names the status of a claim and does not say anything about a voice.

Do not use a stamp to praise ("Best in class") or to fill space. Do not stack two stamps on one figure.
