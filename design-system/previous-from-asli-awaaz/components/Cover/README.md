Cover is the first page: the product name, one line saying what it does, and a drawing of a voice waveform.

The drawing carries one idea and nothing else. A row of vertical bars, 8 units wide at a 16 unit pitch with fully rounded ends, sits directly on the `paper` ground. The bars of real speech are irregular in height and set in `genuine`. The bars of generated speech are all the same height, evenly spaced and set in `synthetic`. The caption under the bars reads "Real speech varies. Generated speech is too even." Set it in `caption` in `ink-soft`.

There is no solid slab, no cap and no coloured block behind the bars. Teal and magenta appear only as the two groups of bars, so the colours keep their meaning. Do not add other shapes in either colour.

The name "Asli Awaaz" is set in `cover-title` in `ink` at the lower left, with the tagline in `caption` below it. The Hindi wordmark appears only here, in `wordmark-hi`. The section counter sits at the left margin on this page (see SectionCounter).
