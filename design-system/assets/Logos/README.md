The mark is seven vertical bars: four of uneven height in `genuine` (a real voice varies) and three of equal height in `synthetic` (a generated voice is too even). Use it as the product mark beside the wordmark "Asli Awaaz" set in `cover-title` or `slide-title` weight 800.

- `logo-mark.svg`: full colour, for `paper` and `paper-deep` grounds. Teal #086B62 and magenta #B0105A.
- `logo-mark-night.svg`: for `night` grounds. Teal #4CCFBF and magenta #FF7BAE.
- `logo-mark-mono-ink.svg`: one ink, #0F1A30, for fax, stamps and single-colour print.
- `logo-mark-mono-paper.svg`: one light ink, #E8EDF3, for dark grounds where colour is unavailable.

Clear space equals one bar width (8 units of 96) on every side. Minimum size 24px high. Do not recolour the two groups to anything else, reorder the bars, or place the mark on a photograph. The files are SVGs shown through `<img>`, so their colours are fixed in the file. `logo-mark.svg` and `logo-mark-night.svg` use two colours, teal and magenta. Only the two mono files are single-colour.
