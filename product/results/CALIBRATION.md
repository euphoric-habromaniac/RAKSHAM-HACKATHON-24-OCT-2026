## Fitted values

- Platt: a = 8.6522, b = -6.9212, p(spoof) = sigmoid(a*x + b), x = softmax spoof probability
- Score mapping: x = service.scorers.spoof_probability([spoof_logit, bonafide_logit]) = sigmoid(spoof_logit - bonafide_logit), exactly the service's raw L2 input
- Thresholds on calibrated probability: weak = 0.825736, strong (Step up) = 0.844083
- Fit method: pooled over all conditions on the fit split; Newton logistic regression of spoof label on x (see score_mapping); pooled thresholds from fit-split genuine quantiles
- Split: seed 0, utterances fit/held-out = 2500/2500; rows fit/held-out = 27500/27500
- Targets: strong threshold set for at most 5.0% genuine at/above it on the fit split; weak threshold 10.0% (our choice, not a team decision)

## Hold

L2 alone never yields Hold under the policy table (service policy): one strong signal gives Step up, Hold needs two strong signals (L2 plus L1 or L4), a failed digit challenge, or other rules. There is therefore no separate L2 'Hold threshold' and no L2-only false-hold rate; the genuine/spoof rates at `strong` and `weak` above are what L2 contributes to those combinations. L1 and L4 are not fitted.

## Caveats

- x is the service's exact raw input (softmax spoof probability from both logits). It is a saturating transform of the logit margin, so Platt on x is a coarser fit than Platt on the margin would be; the cost is visible in ECE/Brier in the hardest conditions.
- Channels are SIMULATED (codec/loss/noise degradation of ASVspoof 2019 LA studio audio), not real bank calls.
- One dataset (ASVspoof 2019 LA eval) and a 5,000-utterance balanced subset (2,500 bona fide / 2,500 spoof) per condition, so the prior is 50% spoof; real calls are overwhelmingly genuine. Probabilities are not prevalence-adjusted, and thresholds chosen on genuine rates are what matter operationally.
- Calibration and thresholds are pooled across all 11 conditions, so the single operating point is a compromise: conditions worse than average (see rows) exceed the 5% genuine step-up target, and easy ones sit under it.
- The 5% target is per accent group in the team spec; ASVspoof has no accent labels, so it is applied to pooled genuine speech here. No fairness (accent gap) evidence is produced.
- The split is by utterance; fit and held-out halves share speakers and attack types, so held-out numbers are optimistic relative to unseen speakers/attacks.
