Read the left table with its condition: three enrolled speakers, one cloner, windows from sentences the model never saw. 98.9% of half-second windows were right; every whole clip was right. This is a feasibility result, not field accuracy, and the stamp says so. The right table is a published detector, not ours: even a strong pretrained model goes from 0.92% EER on clean English to 7.36% on GSM and 15.12% under heavy noise, which is why we test on telephone copies. Never quote 98.9% or 100% without the conditions. About 60 seconds.
