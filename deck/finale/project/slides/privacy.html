Consent and privacy by design

Consent first

Each speaker signed separate permissions for recording, spoof testing and cloning, with 90-day retention.

Audio stays local

The detector runs in the browser. Saved calls stay on the local machine, write-once, with an audio hash.

Evidence you can audit

The service keeps an append-only, hash-chained log per call and records every override with a reason.

High-risk by design

India’s DPDP Act has no separate biometric tier, so we treat voice as high-risk anyway. Main duties start 13 May 2027.

Sources: consent.json files; decision specifications; DLA Piper and HSF on the DPDP Rules, Nov 2025
