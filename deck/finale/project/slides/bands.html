Three bands for every call

Clear

The agent sees no flag and carries on. The normal helpline flow stays unchanged.

Step up

The score is not enough to clear the call, so an extra proof is asked: random digits or a confirmation in the bank app.

Hold

The call is paused, the customer is asked to confirm in the app and an analyst reviews the case.

A failed or unclear challenge becomes Hold, never an automatic block. The agent sees a band and a plain message, never a raw score.

This is the product in one picture. The agent never sees a raw score, only a band and a plain message. Step up is the middle state: ask for extra proof. Hold pauses the call and sends the customer a confirmation in the app. The design rule that matters most: one weak signal never blocks a call, and a failed or unclear challenge becomes Hold, not a block.
