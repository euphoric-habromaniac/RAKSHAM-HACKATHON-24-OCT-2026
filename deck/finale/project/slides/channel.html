Two points. First, banks have not left voice: TRAI moved bank service and transactional calls onto verified 1600 numbers by 1 Jan 2026 precisely because fraud arrives through voice calls. Do not say banks moved to SMS. Second, RBI’s authentication rules allow checks beyond two-factor, which is where a Step up band fits. Then the research: clones fooled a standard speaker verifier up to 82.7% of the time, and a strong detector’s error jumped on unseen tools. That is why we layer checks and never let one score decide. The primary TRAI and RBI texts were not opened in our research pass, so re-check before presenting. About 60 seconds.
