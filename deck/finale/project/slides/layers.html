Five layers, each with a blind spot

LayerQuestion it asksWhat it cannot catch
L1 Speaker matchIs this the enrolled person?A clone close enough to the profile. Clones bypassed ECAPA-TDNN 43 to 83% of the time in research.
L2 Synthetic-speech checkIs the audio AI-generated?Cloners it has not seen. Noisy or compressed lines raise its error.
L3 Digit challengeCan the caller answer random digits on demand?A live voice changer passes it. It stops pre-recorded clones.
L4 Call contextDoes the request look risky: new payee, amount jump, urgency?A calm request from a trusted number.
L5 App confirmationDoes the real customer confirm in the bank app?A customer who confirms under pressure, or has no app.

Our live detector plugs in at L2 for enrolled voices.

Sources: Asli Awaaz design, decision specifications; Hong et al., arXiv 2601.02914

Say what each layer cannot do in the same breath as what it does. L3 stops a pre-recorded clone but a live voice changer passes it, so L2 and L5 cover that case. L1 alone is not enough: in research, open-source clones bypassed a standard speaker verifier 43 to 83% of the time. This week’s work is a concrete L2 candidate for enrolled voices. About 60 seconds.
