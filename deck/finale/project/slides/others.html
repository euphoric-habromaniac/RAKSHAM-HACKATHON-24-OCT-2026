Be straight about overlap. DetectifAI claims on-device detection plus speaker verification, and two other RAKSHAM entries use hold-until-confirmed-in-app logic, so on-device and step up alone are not our edge. What we can say: a consented Hinglish evaluation set with telephone-codec copies, a per-person check with a live challenge, and failure modes stated up front. Do not say we are first, do not say we beat any vendor: no shared test exists, and vendor accuracy figures are self-reported. The only independent multi-vendor test we found (Podonos, 2026, English only) put one major vendor at 53.7% false positives. About 45 seconds.
