Say: today, trust rests on what the caller knows and how the voice sounds, so a cloned voice that gives correct answers gets approved. With Asli Awaaz the call keeps its first three steps, and a risk score beside the call decides what the agent does next. Clear: a genuine customer sees no change. Step up: the agent reads six random digits for one more proof on the same call. Hold: the request is paused, the customer confirms in the bank app and an analyst reviews. The score reaching the agent within seconds is a design target, not a measurement. The Today lane is a typical flow, not a claim about any one bank. Banks differ; we will fit the integration to the bank during pilot discussion.
