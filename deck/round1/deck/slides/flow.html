Say: this picture is the product: a score beside the call, a band for the agent, a second proof only when needed, and a record either way. Clear, Step up and Hold are actions, not colours. A failed or unclear digit challenge becomes Hold, never an automatic block. A live voice changer can pass the digits, so a strong synthetic-speech signal from layer 2 still queues a post-call review even when the challenge passes. The 10 second figure is a design target; we have not measured it yet.
