How a bank adopts it

Assumption: RBI and DPDP fit

Phase 1: Shadow

Scores calls, shows nobody. Scores are compared with confirmed fraud outcomes.

Ends when false Step-up rates are under target in every accent group.

Phase 2: Assist

Agents see the band and message. Nothing is enforced.

Ends when analysts agree with the band on sampled calls at the rate the bank sets.

Phase 3: Step up

Digit challenge and app prompt switch on for the top band. Analyst queue goes live.

Ends when confirmed takeovers fall and complaint rates stay inside the bank’s limit.

Phase 4: Scale

More call types and languages. Retrain when drift alarms fire, not only on a calendar.

Reviewed each quarter by fraud-risk operations.

each step needs the bank’s sign-off

Where it plugs in

Audio from a SIP fork or the contact-centre platform’s media stream (for example Amazon Connect or Genesys Cloud). The band goes to the agent desktop; cases go to the bank’s case system.

Who owns what

Fraud-risk operations owns thresholds and cases. The CISO owns access and audit. IT owns the audio tap.

Friction budget

Clear adds no step. Step up adds six digits; sensitive requests always get it. Only Hold sends an app prompt.

Phases are a proposal for a pilot, not a deployed rollout.
