Say: we can start without confidential bank data. Public spoofing datasets plus our own consented Hinglish recordings, all pushed through a phone-line degradation chain, give a test set that looks like a helpline call. The chain is 8 kHz resampling, G.711, AMR-NB and GSM full rate, Opus at low bitrate, packet loss from 0 to 10%, and background noise at set levels, applied with ffmpeg. ASVspoof 2019 (Edinburgh DataShare) and ASVspoof 5 (Zenodo 14498691) are under the Open Data Commons Attribution License. In-the-Wild: Fraunhofer AISEC says Apache 2.0, the Hugging Face card says CC BY-SA 4.0; attribute under both. Fill [N] and [M] with the planned size of the Hinglish set. The pass line for equal error rate is a statement, not a number: beat the ASVspoof 2019 baseline in every codec. The number comes after the first baseline run; do not quote one before we have measured it. The [x]% target for stepped-up genuine callers is also a placeholder. A1 to A6 are listed with tests in Appendix B.
