Say: today, trust rests on what the caller knows and how the voice sounds, so a cloned voice that gives correct answers gets approved. With Asli Awaaz the call keeps its first three steps, and a score runs beside the call: the caller audio is tapped and layers 1, 2 and 4 score it. The band decides what the agent does next. Clear: a genuine customer sees no change. Step up: the agent reads six random digits for one more proof on the same call. A failed or unclear digit challenge becomes Hold, never an automatic block. Hold: the request is paused, the customer confirms in the bank app and an analyst opens a case. A live voice changer can pass the digits, so a strong synthetic-speech signal from layer 2 still queues a post-call review even when the challenge passes. Every path writes a record. Clear, Step up and Hold are actions with their own shapes; colour is a second cue. The 10 second figure is a design target; we have not measured it yet. The Today lane is a typical flow, not a claim about any one bank. Banks differ; we will fit the integration to the bank during pilot discussion.
