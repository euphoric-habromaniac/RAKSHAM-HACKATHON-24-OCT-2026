Say: the system in one picture. The service sits inside the bank’s network and listens to a copy of the call audio. Scoring and storage stay in the bank’s network; the bank app and the customer’s phone are outside it, and the prompt reaches the phone through the bank app. Ingest feeds three paths: ASR (faster-whisper), whose transcript goes to L4 for urgency words, and L1 and L2 directly. The bank’s device, payee and amount flags go straight into L4 over an API, not through the audio path. The policy engine turns the scores into Clear, Step up or Hold. Only Step up starts L3, the digit challenge; its pass or fail comes back to policy. Outputs go to four places: the agent desktop gets the band and message, the bank app gets a prompt on Hold only, the analyst console gets the case, and the bank’s case system and SIEM get the alert. The fraud-head dashboard is a separate screen; it reads the audit log, and threshold changes need two approvers. Stores: L1 reads voiceprints (with the consent record); each call writes evidence and the append-only audit log. Sizes and models are the ones on pitch page 5.
