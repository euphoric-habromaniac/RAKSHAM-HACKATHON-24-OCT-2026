What an attacker tries

Replay of a pre-made clone

Random digits on Step up; reply-time check.

Live voice changer

L2 on every window; post-call review if it misses.

Noise or codec tricks

Train on degraded audio; add new generators; red-team before a pilot.

Probing the system

Attempt caps; the caller learns only that an extra proof was asked, never which check fired or the score.

Misuse of voiceprints or evidence

Role-based access; encrypted with bank-held keys, TLS in transit; audit log.

Evidence-clip retention is the bank’s policy
