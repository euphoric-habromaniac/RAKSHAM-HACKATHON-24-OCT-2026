Time budget is a design target

Architecture page 3 of 3

Say: a case has five states and four roles. Only the analyst can release or escalate; the fraud head can change thresholds only with a second person. The time budget is a design target, drawn as two bars to one scale against the 10 s target: about 6.9 s on GPU and 8.9 s on CPU, before network and UI delay. The audio window itself is the largest part, so model speed matters less than it looks. Nothing here has been measured yet.
